About Crop Advisor

Crop and Fertilizer Advisor is an intelligent agricultural recommendation system that helps farmers and agricultural professionals make data-driven decisions about crop selection and fertilizer application.

Our platform combines machine learning algorithms with real-time weather data to provide personalized recommendations based on your specific soil conditions and climate.

Key Features

Crop Recommendation

Get AI-powered suggestions for the best crops to grow based on your soil and climate conditions.

Fertilizer Advice

Receive optimal fertilizer recommendations tailored to your crop and soil type.

Weather Integration

Real-time weather data automatically fills in temperature, humidity, and rainfall information.

History Tracking

Keep track of all your past recommendations and analyze patterns over time.

Our Mission

We aim to empower farmers with technology-driven insights to optimize crop yields, reduce resource waste, and promote sustainable agricultural practices. By leveraging machine learning and real-time data, we make precision agriculture accessible to everyone.

Technology

Our system uses advanced machine learning models trained on thousands of agricultural data points. We employ Decision Tree and Random Forest algorithms to analyze soil nutrients (N, P, K), pH levels, temperature, humidity, and rainfall patterns to provide accurate recommendations.
